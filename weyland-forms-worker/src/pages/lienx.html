LIEN WAIVERS

LienX

Where a state prints its lien waiver forms in its statutes, a waiver counts only on that form. LienX fills your state's statutory form word for word, from the statute on the legislature's own site, with the job's owner, customer, amount and dates. It covers Arizona, Nevada and Florida today. A state with statutory forms LienX doesn't carry yet (California, Texas and others) is refused instead of getting a generic waiver that may not hold. Any other state gets a general-form waiver labelled as one. Previewing is free; the PDF comes with LienX, the suite or the $100 first submittal.
