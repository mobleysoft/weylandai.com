PriceX

PriceX takes the list price of each item from the maker's own price book on file, matching the catalogue number and finish on your schedule. It prices the SubX schedule at your multipliers: every line times the openings that use its set. Each price names its book and says whether it matched exactly or is a base price with options not priced. An item with no price book on file is listed as not priced and left out of the total; nothing is estimated. Lookups and on-screen pricing are free; the CSV export comes with PriceX, the suite or the $100 first submittal.
