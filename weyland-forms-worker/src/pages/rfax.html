RFaX

RFaX reads the door and hardware schedule SubX read from your plans and finds what a hardware sub has to ask:

  • door groups whose hardware set isn't in the hardware schedule;
  • fire-rated openings whose set lists no closer or no latching device;
  • openings with no size;
  • items with no manufacturer named.

Each one becomes a numbered RFI listing the affected openings, with the schedule pages they were read from attached. You can track it until it's answered. Writing and tracking are free; the PDF comes with RFaX, the suite or the $100 first submittal.
