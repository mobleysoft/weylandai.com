## First (maker) comment — draft for John to edit in his own voice
Hi Product Hunt — John here, founder of WeylandAI.

WeylandAI is seven working tools for commercial subcontractors that share one continuous project record, from the first public bid to the final walkthrough: HuntX (opportunity discovery), TakeOffX (machine-vision takeoffs), SubX (submittal extraction), CutsheetX (catalog matching with citations back to the source document), PropX (margin-protected proposals), MeetingX (the room), and SightX (a real-time 3D field twin).

A few things we did deliberately:
- No sign-up wall. The site mints an ephemeral session through our own auth subsidiary, authfor.com, so you can run the real HuntX and CutsheetX demos on the homepage without an account. Upgrade when you want to keep your work.
- No fake demos. Every demo on the page hits the real backend. When something fails, you see the real error, not a canned success.
- The site itself is an artifact in SightX's world. It opens in first person with a dossier in your hand; raise it and it is the website. The opening sequence illustrates a cosmology hypothesis I am developing, labeled as a hypothesis, next to the parts that are observed.

Privacy and terms live on consenta.cc, our consent and compliance venture, and every user has a real data-rights request path there.

Ask me anything — about takeoffs, about the twin, or about why a folder.
