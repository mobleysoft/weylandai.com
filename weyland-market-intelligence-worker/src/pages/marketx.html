How much door work your city is permitting, and who is building it. MarketX reads the building permits Chicago, New York, Los Angeles, Austin, San Francisco and Seattle publish: commercial and multifamily work of $250,000 or more, new buildings and alterations, single-family work left out. Each permit is read for its building use and for whether the work is likely to include doors (new buildings and interior work are; facade, roofing and mechanical-only work are not). You see the permitted value by month against the year before, the mix by building use, the largest and newest projects, and the general contractors and owners behind them, with the open public bids in the same state from HuntX.
