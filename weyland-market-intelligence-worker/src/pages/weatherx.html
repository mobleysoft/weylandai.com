WeatherX

For each job (your PropX proposals), WeatherX shows the next 7 days from the National Weather Service, with the install risk for door and frame work: rain, wind on glass and storefront leaves, snow and ice, and cold for sealants and thresholds. It also keeps a daily log of what the nearest NWS station observed at the job: temperatures, wind and gusts, precipitation and conditions. That log is your record when weather delays the work. Viewing is free; the log export comes with WeatherX, the suite or the $100 first submittal.
