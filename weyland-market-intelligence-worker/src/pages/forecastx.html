ForecastX

Monthly cash flow across your contracts (PropX proposals plus the change orders ChangeOrdX marks approved). For each job, ForecastX models billing month by month and the payment that follows after the job's terms. It holds back retainage and releases it after the last bill, and it pays for material on your supplier's terms. The result is the net cash each month, the running total, and the month you're lowest. Viewing is free; the CSV comes with ForecastX, the suite or the $100 first submittal.
