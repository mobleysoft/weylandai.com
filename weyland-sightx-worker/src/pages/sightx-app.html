A SubX session is laid out on its own floor plan: SubX finds the plan sheets by their title blocks, reads the rooms they label, and ties each door tag to its schedule mark; the door stands where its tag is drawn, in that room, at the sheet's printed scale. With no plan (a pasted schedule, the sample sheet, or a set whose plans name no A sheet a plan) the doors stand in schedule order along one schematic corridor, and the page says so. Sizes, materials, ratings and hardware are the schedule's. A door the schedule gives no size for is drawn 3'-0" x 7'-0" and says so; a set with no parts listed is drawn bare. Walls and door swings are not read from the plan yet, so none are drawn there.
